App Review Notes - ReceiptBox 1.4.0 (57)

No login required. Tap "Scan" on the main screen and allow camera access to
scan a receipt. The scan is saved as a PDF in the app's own Documents folder
and listed newest first. The sort setting is stored locally.

The app has no network features, no analytics SDKs, no ads, and does not
track users. All data stays on device.
